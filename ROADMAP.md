# 🧭 Roadmap

> The full, current version is a page:
> **[psanctuary.github.io/changelog-astro-public/roadmap](https://psanctuary.github.io/changelog-astro-public/roadmap)**.
> What's done is in the [changelog](https://psanctuary.github.io/changelog-astro-public/changelog).

psanctuary is the church's decentralized, playful side on GitHub. This is where it's headed, in
rough order. There are no dates: each step happens when it's ready, and lands in the changelog first.

## Next

- **Give from a crypto wallet** on [`/donate/`](https://psanctuary.github.io/donate/): the Connect
  button is there now, as a preview; giving opens once the church designates a treasury address of
  its own (never a placeholder). The [pledge of support](https://psanctuary.github.io/pledge/)
  already uses the same connection.
- **Thanks you can claim yourself:** a contributor could add their name to the contribution ledger by
  signing with a wallet, with no personal details involved.

## Stewardship and succession

So these pages outlive any one person (today drasticstatic, Christopher W, owns or administers the
organization and holds the tokens that publish it):

- [ ] At least two owners for the GitHub organization, from the church's own people
- [ ] Publishing through org-owned automation (a GitHub App, or an org bot account), not personal tokens
- [ ] A token register, a renewal runbook, and written hand-over steps
- [ ] Exports and backups of the repositories and anything outside GitHub, on a schedule
- [ ] Wallet connect: Psanctuary's own Reown project (a one-line `org.js` change, `give.reownProjectId`), before any treasury address is published

## Later, and still being explored

- **Tokens of gratitude.** Soulbound, non-transferable thank-yous from Christopher W's
  gratitude-token project: one stays in the wallet it's given to, can't be sold, sent on or traded,
  and isn't money. A deeper, optional step beyond a signed pledge. Still being tested; no dates.
- **A network of our own.** Profiles and posts on open protocols like Lens and Farcaster, paired with
  a Psanctuary DAO where members propose and decide together. Privacy first. A direction, not a
  promise.
- **No Discord, on purpose.** Psanctuary once had a Discord; Eric O closed it so the community
  gathers in one place, on Mighty Networks. A Discord may only ever return as a help-desk back end
  most people would never see, unless a DAO or something new grows here. Time will tell.
