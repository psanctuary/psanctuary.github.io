A quiet home on GitHub

Psanctuary is an inter-tradition church built around a sincere sacred mushroom practice. Its real home is psanctuary.org, where Sunday service, membership, courses, and events all live. This GitHub organization is newer and much quieter: a place to keep the church's technical work, a public changelog, and a library of how things are built, out in the open.

The church holds two GitHub organizations, psanctuarychurch and psanctuary, so the name stays safe while the work takes shape. You're in psanctuary; its sibling is psanctuarychurch. How the two will divide the work hasn't been decided yet.

What's changed

The AstroLog keeps a plain-language record of what's been built, newest first. Each entry has its own page, and its own preview card when you share it.

Who made the changes

Every entry credits whoever did the work, by first name. Eric and Alana are here early, to show how the record will look as more of the community takes part.
